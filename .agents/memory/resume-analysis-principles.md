---
name: Resume analysis principles
description: Durable product constraints for the resume scoring tool.
---

The analyzer must treat the score as a directional communication signal, not an ATS, interview, or hiring guarantee. Skills count most when they are tied to visible work, projects, research, open-source contributions, or validated outcomes; missing evidence should remain a gap rather than becoming an invented claim.

**Why:** The supplied evaluator instructions explicitly prohibit guarantees and fabricated experience, and evidence-first feedback is the core trust promise of the product.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between role readiness and job-specific matching, keep parser risks visible, and derive every positive recommendation from content that can be found in the resume or job description.
